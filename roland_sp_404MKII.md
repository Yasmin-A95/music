## Settings I changed to attempt to make the mic sound better

- Exited Looper Mode: Pressed [SHIFT] + [LOOP] to escape the stuck recording loop that was locking up your screen, SUB PAD, and BUS FX buttons.
- Unmuted the Bus: Pressed [SHIFT] + [BUS FX] to ensure the audio lines weren't muted.
- Switched Routing to Mix: Changed the internal audio path from "ExtIn" (which cuts off microphone monitoring) to "Mix" using the RESONANCE knob on the record settings screen.
- • Turned Off Input FX Bypass: Entered the Input FX screen to make sure it wasn't bypassing your settings.
- Maxed Input Level: Held [SHIFT] + [EXT SOURCE] and turned the [CTRL 3] knob all the way clockwise to 127 to ensure the microphone's digital mixer channel is at maximum volume.
- Boosted Global Outputs: Navigated to the UTILITY > SYSTEM > GAIN menu and raised Phones Out to +12dB to drive a louder signal out of the unit.
- • Engaged Input Compressor: Turned on the Compressor (Cmp) effect specifically on the Input FX layer (which isolates it to only affect your mic, leaving your beats clean) with these settings:
  • Sustain: 70 to 80 (to aggressively pull up quiet vocal levels)
  • Attack: 20 to 35
  • Ratio: 50 to 65
  • Level: 75 to 90 (to send a hot signal out to your amplifier)

## Clear All Active Effects (Bypass)

If you have effects running on Bus 1 or Bus 2, you can quickly bypass them:
• Press the [BUS FX] button. Look at the screen—if Bus 1 or Bus 2 shows an active effect name, hold [SHIFT] and press the [BUS FX] button to completely Mute or turn off the effect processing engine.
• Alternatively, you can turn off any of the physical top effect buttons ([FILTER+DRIVE], [RESONATOR], [DELAY], etc.) by pressing them so their lights go dark.

## Reset the Bus Routing to Factory Default

To ensure your microphone and pads are routed normally (going through Bus 1 and Bus 2 sequentially without weird parallel routing tricks):

1. Hold down [SHIFT] and press Pad 16 (EFX SETTING).
2. Use the [CTRL 3] knob (the far right knob) to switch to the "OTHER" tab at the top of the screen.
3. Look at Bus Topology (Bus Routing structure). Ensure it is set to Type A (this is the default factory layout where Bus 1 flows cleanly straight into Bus 2).

## Send your Microphone to Bus 2

1. Hold down [SHIFT] and press Pad 16 (EFX SETTING).
2. Turn the [CTRL 3] knob (the far right top knob) to highlight the "OTHER" tab on-screen.
3. Use the [VALUE] knob to scroll down and highlight Input Bus.
4. Click the [VALUE] knob down, turn it to select BUS2, and click it again to save.

# Step 2: Ensure your Samples stay on Bus 1

1. Look at the [BUS FX] button. Press it until the screen shows "BUS 1" at the top.
2. Make sure all effect lights ([FILTER], [DELAY], etc.) are turned off for Bus 1. This ensures your beats pass through completely clean and untouched.

# Step 3: Add an Effect ONLY to your Microphone

Now that your mic lives exclusively on Bus 2, any effect you turn on while looking at Bus 2 will only affect your vocals:

1. Tap the [BUS FX] button so the screen switches to show "BUS 2" at the top.
2. Select any effect you want to apply to your voice (for example, press [DELAY] or use the [MFX] knob to find a nice vocal Reverb).
3. Speak into your microphone—you will hear the effect beautifully layered over your vocals, while hitting your performance pads will play your backing samples completely crisp, dry, and unaffected!

## How To

- Clean up amplifier background fuzz: Turn down the amplifier's Pre-Gain/Drive knob (set to 1 or 2) and use the Post-Gain/Master volume knob on the amp to control the room volume.
- Avoid mic clipping: Keep the physical front-panel GAIN knob on the sampler around 10 o'clock or 12 o'clock (noon) so the sensitive new microphone does not distort or blink red.
- Record vocals over a beat: Press [REC], select an empty blinking pad, start your backing track pad, perform into the mic, and press [REC] again to stop and save the sample.
